# LMG Video Grid – release notes

## 1.0.1 – 2 Nov 2026

**New**
- The film titles in "365 ways to travel the world" under the articles no longer start with a large gold first letter. At that size it read as a typo ("B oracay", "J apan beyond…"). Titles now read normally: "Boracay — Shangri-La of the Philippines".

**What you do differently**
- Nothing. Install over 1.0 on both sites (Plugins → Add New Plugin → Upload Plugin → Replace current with uploaded), then purge the cache.

**Good to know**
- Only the look of the titles changes; the films, their order and the "Watch more on YouTube" link are as before.
- Tested by applying the same change to a live article in a browser. Not tested installed.

**Older content**
- Applies to every article with the film block from installation on. Nothing in the articles changes.
