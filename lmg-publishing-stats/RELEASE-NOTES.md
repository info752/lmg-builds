# LMG Publishing Stats – release notes

## 1.1 – 27 Nov 2026

**New**
- Translations made with LMG Translator now count as published content. The Publishing Overview box on the Dashboard and the Publishing page count them in Today, 7 days and 30 days, and "Last published" can be a translation (shown with its language, e.g. [DE]).
- Under the numbers, the box says how many of them are translations, per language (e.g. EN 7, DE 2).
- The Publishing page has a new table: translations published in the last 30 days, by language. The monthly bars and the recent list include translations too.
- Translations are dated by the day they went live, so a translation published yesterday counts as yesterday's content, not as the date of the Danish original.

**What you do differently**
- Nothing.

**Good to know**
- Drafts still counts article drafts only; translation drafts are shown beside it as "+N translation drafts", so the queue number keeps its meaning.
- "Today" means the last 24 hours, in the site's own time zone (1.0 mixed server and site time).
- Tested with a mock-up of WordPress, not yet on a live site.
- LeisureIntel's own overview page uses these same numbers, so it shows translations too once this version is installed.

**Older content**
- Applies automatically: translations already live are counted from installation on, by their go-live date.
