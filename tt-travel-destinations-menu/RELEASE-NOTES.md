# TT Travel Destinations Menu – release notes

## 1.4 – 16 November 2026

**New**
- English pages whose breadcrumb still carries a Danish name (for example "Caribien") are now placed in the right dropdown. The menu no longer relies on the name alone: it follows the breadcrumb's link to the Danish page, finds the English translation of it, and puts the page under the dropdown entry for that translation (Caribbean).

**What you do differently**
- After installing: Tools → Travel Destinations Menu → **Sync now**. The ten Caribbean islands (Dominican Republic, Curaçao, Cuba, Grenada, Aruba, Bermuda, Cayman Islands, Anguilla, The Bahamas, Barbados) should then say "Added under Caribbean".

**Good to know**
- Works only for regions that have a published English translation on traveltalk.travel; others keep the "Not added" line as before.
- Tested with sample menu data; not yet tested on the live menu. Watch the first Sync now report.
- The breadcrumb itself on those pages still shows "Caribien"; that is a Translator matter, handled separately.

**Older content**
- Catch-up: pages left out by 1.3 are placed at the next sync (Sync now, or the daily check). Entries already in the menu are not touched.
